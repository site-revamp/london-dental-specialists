The specialist approach

Care that looks at the bigger picture.

Some dental concerns benefit from input across different specialities. A coordinated approach can help bring the relevant expertise together when planning care around an individual's needs.

At London Dental Specialists, the practice describes a collaborative, multidisciplinary approach across areas including implants, gum health, orthodontics and root canal treatment.
